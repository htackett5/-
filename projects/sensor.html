OPTICAL HEART-RATE SENSOR

Infrared sensing, signal conditioning and a comparator-driven output.

WATCH THE DEMO

DESIGN / PROCESS / RESULTS

BEHIND THE BUILD.

THE DESIGN

Developed an infrared sensing circuit using an IR LED and phototransistor to detect changes in received light.

THE CHALLENGE & APPROACH

Conditioned the small sensor signal using cascaded high-pass and low-pass amplification stages with a nominal combined gain near 206 V/V. Added comparator hysteresis and MOSFET switching for a visible LED output.

THE RESULT

Tested the sensing, amplification, filtering, and switching subsystems independently before integration. The project demonstrates analog signal conditioning and staged hardware testing.
