ANALOG ELECTRONICS / AUDIO

THREE-BAND AUDIO EQUALIZER

A complete analog signal chain, designed, measured, debugged and improved.

WATCH THE DEMO

DESIGN / PROCESS / RESULTS

BEHIND THE BUILD.

THE DESIGN

Designed low-, mid-, and high-frequency filter paths around 320 Hz and 3.2 kHz, combining adjustable bands through op-amp stages to drive an 8 Ω speaker.

THE CHALLENGE & APPROACH

Interstage loading limited output performance. After identifying the issue, I added a buffer amplifier to reduce loading and improve the circuit’s ability to drive the speaker.

THE RESULT

Achieved 483 mW into an 8 Ω load, exceeding the 400 mW requirement by approximately 21%.
